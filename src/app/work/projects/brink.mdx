## One map, seven sets of orders

Brink is a geopolitical strategy game set in a fictional 2030s. Each player controls a power competing for influence in twenty swing states. Everyone submits orders in secret, then the rules engine resolves the turn simultaneously.

## Testing the balance

Military and civil control occupy separate slots on the board. A move that helps one power can also raise the crisis level for everyone. Bot games made it possible to simulate those incentives repeatedly while tuning the rules. Try a practice game or invite others to a table.
