## The brief

Travel Counsellors is a £1.5bn, PE-backed travel group built around more than two thousand independent travel counsellors and the people who support them. In 2025 the business created its first AI Innovation Partner role and I took it on. The remit was deliberately open: work out where AI actually earns its place across the group, and then get it into production rather than leaving it in a deck.

## How I work the problem

Most of the job is choosing. Plenty of things can be demoed with a language model; far fewer survive contact with a live business, its systems and the people who have to use them every day. So each candidate use case goes through the same loop: sit with the team that owns the workflow, find the moment that costs them time or money, build the smallest version that could work, put it in front of real users, and measure what changes before deciding whether to scale it or stop.

## What has shipped so far

- **Voice agents for recruitment.** Conversational agents that screen and schedule candidates, wired into Zoom Contact Center so they sit inside the tooling the recruitment team already uses rather than beside it.
- **AI customer and app support agents.** Agents that resolve routine support queries for customers and for counsellors using the company's apps, and hand off cleanly when a human is needed.
- **A ChatGPT app for travel matching.** An app that helps people describe the trip they have in mind and matches them with the right counsellor, meeting customers where they already are.

## Telling the story internally

Adoption is a communication problem as much as a technical one. I gave the keynote at TC Together in Manchester, walking counsellors and senior leadership through where the group is taking AI, what it will and will not do, and how it fits the way they already work.
