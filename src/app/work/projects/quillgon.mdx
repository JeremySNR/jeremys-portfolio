## Remembering a whole novel

A change to one chapter can unravel a detail established ten chapters earlier. Quillgon keeps the manuscript, scene plans and character information together so the author can follow those threads across a book.

## Editing on your terms

Use AI to propose revisions, review the changes and decide what belongs in the manuscript. The workspace keeps the project files locally and lets the writer take a draft through more than one round of editing.
